About Me

Curious mind.
Determined heart.

I'm Ayaana — a student at The Shri Ram School, Aravali with a deep love for Economics and Psychology. I'm fascinated by why people behave the way they do and how those choices ripple through societies and markets.

Outside the classroom, I build things that matter. From a soil-testing device presented at the United Nations in Geneva, to an app that connects 25,000 villagers to their local leaders — I believe technology and empathy can solve real problems.

I've had the privilege of learning at Harvard, BITS Goa, and the Wadhwani Foundation, and I volunteer in education and waste management. When I'm not studying or building, you'll find me singing, painting, or on the badminton court.
